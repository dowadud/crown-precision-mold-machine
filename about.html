About Crown Precision

Built On
Industrial Mold Experience

Crown Precision Mold & Machine supports tire manufacturers with specialized mold repair, refurbishment, cleaning, CNC machining, inspection, and embedded mold shop support from two production facilities: Rainbow City, Alabama and Bahia, Brazil.

Operating Model

CPM&M is built around production continuity. The company focuses on repair decisions, documented process control, mold lifecycle extension, and practical speed for plants that cannot afford avoidable tooling downtime.

The footprint supports manufacturers that need regional mold service capacity across North America and Brazil.
